Which Came First? · a story

Time sorts

The daily game Which Came First? draws from famous things in kinds: films, albums, books, discoveries and more. Each dot here is one of them.

Fame here means one thing: how many Wikipedias have an article on it. Everything in the pool cleared a bar for its kind. So these are not all the albums ever made. They are the albums people still write about.

Among those, are the older ones more famous? For some kinds, clearly. For others, not at all.
