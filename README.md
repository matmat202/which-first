# Which Came First?

**<https://matmat202.github.io/which-first/>**

Two famous things, one question: which came first? The telephone or photography? Titanic or
the Higgs boson? Toyota or Batman? Ten a day. The first pair is centuries apart and the
last is a year or two. After each answer you see both years on a timeline. Everyone gets the
same ten, a new set arrives at your midnight, and your streak and results stay in your
browser.

## Where the things come from

[Wikidata](https://www.wikidata.org/) (CC0). The pool is about 1,300 inventions and
discoveries, films, books, video games, albums, songs, paintings, TV series, companies and
landmarks. They are the ones with articles in the most Wikipedias, which is a rough measure
of how well known something is. Each one gets one year:

- **Inventions and discoveries:** Wikidata's "time of discovery or invention". Anything with
  two different years is left out.
- **Films, books, games, albums, songs:** the first publication or release date. Casablanca
  has three (1942, 1943, 1947), and the earliest is the year it came out.
- **Companies, landmarks, paintings:** inception, when there's no publication date.

Some things are left out on purpose: places "discovered" by Europeans, the thousands of
asteroids and galaxies, anything whose year is only known to the decade, and anything whose
Wikidata dates contradict each other. Only 24 chemical elements are kept, because every
Wikipedia has an article on every element. Descriptions are Wikidata's with any year taken
out, since "1997 film directed by James Cameron" would give the answer away.

Some years are arguable (when was the computer invented?), and Wikidata is edited by
volunteers. The year shown is Wikidata's.
